## Engineering Principles

### Reliable over impressive

Critical business logic should be predictable, testable and explainable.

### AI where it adds value

LLMs are useful for classification, extraction and summarization. Deterministic rules remain preferable where business decisions require predictable behavior.

### Human-in-the-loop when needed

Automation should know when not to make the final decision.

### Provider-independent architecture

Integrations should be replaceable without rebuilding the entire business process.

### No secrets in repositories

Public implementations use synthetic data, mock credentials and `.env.example` files.
